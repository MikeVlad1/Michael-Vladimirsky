Michael Vladimirsky

ElectroFlow is my IPPD senior design project, sponsored by Arthrex: a new bipolar RF probe for open orthopedic surgery that runs on an existing Arthrex RF console. I am the team lead (a team of 7) and the electrical lead, and I led the electrical design, including the coagulation (coag) PCB.

Medical devices have to limit how much stray current can flow through the patient, so leakage current was a key requirement. Our measured leakage was 40 mA and 20 mA, well under the 150 mA limit, and we delivered 3 working prototypes against the 2 that were required.
