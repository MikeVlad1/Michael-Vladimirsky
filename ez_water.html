Michael Vladimirsky

Research with the Bioelectrical Communication Lab at UF on exclusion-zone (EZ) water. I built a measurement fixture with micrometer-level positioning and infrared LEDs at different wavelengths, so that a change in the EZ can be detected optically.

To drive the LEDs I designed an ESP32-based PCB with MOSFET-driven IR LED arrays, and I wrote Python pipelines to process the measurements. The 3D-printed LED and aperture holder for this setup is on the 3D Print Projects page.
